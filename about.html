About Me

Hello! My name is Rohan Senapati, and I am a student at Emerald High School with interests in mathematics, computer science, machine learning, and scientific research.

From a young age, I have been interested in advanced mathematics and competitive problem solving. While working through increasingly challenging mathematics problems, I eventually encountered the field of complex numbers, whose elegance and intricacies fascinated me. As I continued exploring these ideas, I naturally became drawn toward fractal geometry and the Mandelbrot set.

What began as curiosity about mathematical structures gradually expanded into broader interests in computational mathematics, programming, research, and scientific exploration. I enjoy combining mathematical thinking with programming to investigate challenging problems, build computational models, create visualizations, and explore connections between theory and practical applications.

My work has included research in fractal geometry and computational mathematics, exploration of differential geometry, machine learning coursework, programming projects, and mathematical modeling. These experiences strengthened my interests in mathematical discovery, computational thinking, and interdisciplinary problem solving.

Beyond academics, I enjoy tutoring students, studying advanced mathematics, participating in research projects, and exploring new areas of computer science and artificial intelligence.

This portfolio highlights some of the projects, research, and experiences that have shaped my growth as a student, researcher, and programmer.
